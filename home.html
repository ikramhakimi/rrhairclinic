HOW WE WORK

A structured certification workflow your project team can follow.

Green building certification becomes difficult when responsibilities, evidence, and credit requirements are unclear. Mampan keeps the process traceable — with defined stages, clear deliverables, and practical next actions.

STEP 01
Project Pathway Review
We review your project type, development stage, target framework, timeline, and available documentation to define the most realistic certification pathway.
STEP 02
Credit Gap Analysis
Your current design is mapped against rating criteria to identify achievable credits, missing evidence, technical risks, and priority actions.
STEP 03
Design & Team Coordination
We work with architects, engineers, contractors, and owners to align design decisions with certification requirements before issues become costly.
STEP 04
Submission Documentation
Credit reports, drawings, calculations, evidence registers, and supporting files are prepared and reviewed for assessor-ready submission.
STEP 05
Assessor Response Support
When clarifications or assessor comments arise, we help your team respond with the right evidence, explanation, and technical justification.

Flexible engagement. Mampan can step in at concept stage, mid-design, during construction, or late documentation catch-up. Each engagement is scoped around your project’s current position and what needs to happen next.

ABOUT MAMPAN

Built to make green certification
clearer, faster, and more manageable.

OUR MISSION

To help project teams turn green building requirements into clear decisions, coordinated evidence, and credible certification outcomes.

Green building certification in Malaysia can feel complex because it sits between design, engineering, documentation, construction, and assessor review. Mampan exists to bring structure to that process — helping developers and project teams understand what matters, what is missing, and what needs to happen next.

We believe sustainability should be practical enough to implement, technical enough to stand up to review, and clear enough for every stakeholder to follow.

OUR VISION

A built environment where better-performing buildings become the normal way to build.

Our vision is a Malaysia where green building certification is not treated as a late-stage checkbox, but as part of better project planning from the beginning.

Mampan supports that shift by making the certification process more transparent, more structured, and more achievable for the teams shaping our cities.
