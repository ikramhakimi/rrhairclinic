How we work

A certification workflow your project team can actually follow.

Green building certification becomes difficult when responsibilities, evidence, and credit requirements are unclear. Mampan keeps the process traceable with defined stages, clear deliverables, and practical next actions.

Flexible engagement

We can step in at concept stage, mid-design, during construction, or late documentation catch-up.

Step 01

Project pathway review

We review project type, stage, target framework, timeline, and available documentation.

Step 02

Credit gap analysis

Current design is mapped against rating criteria to identify credits, missing evidence, and risks.

Step 03

Design and team coordination

We align architects, engineers, contractors, and owners before issues become costly.

Step 04

Submission documentation

Credit reports, drawings, calculations, evidence registers, and supporting files are reviewed.

Step 05

Assessor response support

We help respond with the right evidence, explanation, and technical justification.

About Mampan

Green certification made legible across design, engineering, and evidence.

Our mission

To help project teams turn green building requirements into clear decisions, coordinated evidence, and credible certification outcomes.

Green building certification in Malaysia can feel complex because it sits between design, engineering, documentation, construction, and assessor review. Mampan exists to bring structure to that process, helping developers and project teams understand what matters, what is missing, and what needs to happen next.

Our vision

A built environment where better-performing buildings become the normal way to build.

Our vision is a Malaysia where green building certification is part of better project planning from the beginning, not a late-stage checkbox.
