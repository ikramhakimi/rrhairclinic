## Landing Page Funnel

The entire page should follow this customer journey:

**I have a problem**
↓
**RR understands my problem**
↓
**RR has a clear approach**
↓
**RR has proof**
↓
**There are treatment options for my condition**
↓
**I trust the doctor / clinic**
↓
**I understand what happens next**
↓
**My questions are answered**
↓
**I am ready to start**

## 1. Hero

### Purpose

Create a strong first impression, establish RR Hair Clinic's credibility, and immediately communicate what the clinic can help with.

The hero should also support returning or high-intent visitors who are already ready to contact the clinic.

### Content

* Strong SEO-focused H1
* Supporting subheadline with relevant search keywords
* Primary CTA: **Start Free Hair Check**
* Secondary / Quick Action: **WhatsApp**
* Trust signals & credibility

  * Doctor-led treatment
  * Patient results
  * Clinic credentials
  * Location / Malaysia presence
* Optional microcopy below CTA

  * Free
  * No obligation
  * Private
  * Quick response

---

## 2. Problem — Hair Loss

### Purpose

Help visitors immediately recognise their own hair loss condition and feel that RR understands the problem they are experiencing.

This section should move the visitor from:

**“I have a hair problem.”**

to:

**“RR understands what I am dealing with.”**

### Content

* SEO-focused section headline
* Short supporting subheadline
* 3 key struggles caused by hair loss

  * Changes in appearance
  * Loss of confidence
  * Uncertainty about what treatment is suitable
* Hair Loss Types carousel

  * Male Pattern Baldness
  * Female Hair Loss
  * Receding Hairline
  * Thinning Hair
  * Crown Hair Loss
  * Hair Shedding
* Optional CTA:
  **Find Out What's Causing Your Hair Loss**

---

## 3. Our Approach

### Purpose

Explain how RR approaches hair loss differently and how treatment decisions are made.
The goal is to communicate that RR does not simply recommend the same treatment to every patient.

### Content

* Strong positioning statement / large quote
* Short explanation of RR's treatment philosophy
* 3–4 core principles

  * Understand the cause first
  * Recommend treatment based on condition
  * Preserve existing hair where possible
  * Focus on natural-looking outcomes
* Supporting statistics or credibility points
* Optional doctor quote

### Key Message

**The right treatment starts with understanding the cause of hair loss.**

---

## 4. Real Results

### Purpose

Build trust through visible proof that RR has successfully treated patients with similar concerns.

This should become one of the strongest credibility sections on the page.

### Content

* SEO-friendly section headline
* Supporting subheadline
* Before / After carousel
* Treatment or condition label for each case
* Treatment timeline where appropriate
* Patient testimonials
* Optional short patient journey / case context
* CTA:
  **See More Patient Results**

---

## 5. Treatment Pathways

### Purpose

Show visitors the treatment options available without overwhelming them with medical information.

The focus should be on helping visitors understand that different hair-loss conditions require different treatment pathways.

### Content

* SEO-focused section headline
* Supporting subheadline

### Primary Treatment Cards

Highlight the main treatments RR wants to prioritise.

Examples:

* Hair Transplant
* Sapphire FUE
* PRP / Regenerative Hair Treatment
* Medical Hair Treatment

### Supporting Treatments

Additional treatments can be shown in a smaller list or secondary cards.

### Each Treatment Card Should Include

* Treatment name
* What problem it helps with
* Short description
* Link to treatment page

### Optional CTA

**Explore All Treatments**

---

## 6. Meet Your Doctor / Why RR

### Purpose

Increase trust by showing who will be responsible for the patient's treatment and why patients should consider RR.

For a medical service, the doctor should be one of the main credibility assets of the website.

### Content

* Doctor profile
* Qualifications
* Experience
* Treatment philosophy
* Professional memberships / certifications
* Clinic credentials
* Key reasons to choose RR

Examples:

* Doctor-led treatment planning
* Individualised hairline design
* Natural-result philosophy
* Transparent treatment recommendations
* Local follow-up and aftercare

### Optional Supporting Content

* Short doctor video
* Patient testimonial
* Clinic photography

---

## 7. How It Works

### Purpose

Remove uncertainty by clearly showing visitors what happens after they contact RR.

The process should feel easy, low-risk and straightforward.

### Content

* Section headline
* Short supporting subheadline

### Suggested Flow

#### Step 01 — Start Your Hair Check

Answer a few questions or send photos of your current hair condition.

#### Step 02 — Get Your Assessment

RR reviews the condition and identifies possible treatment options.

#### Step 03 — Speak With RR

Discuss your concerns, expectations and available treatment pathways.

#### Step 04 — Get Your Treatment Plan

Receive a personalised recommendation based on your condition.

### CTA

**Start Free Hair Check**

### Supporting Microcopy

Free · Private · No obligation

---

## 8. Products

### Purpose

Introduce RR's supporting hair-care products without distracting visitors from the clinic's primary treatment services.

This section should be positioned as complementary care rather than the main solution.

### Content

* Section headline
* Short subheadline
* Featured products
* Product purpose / benefit
* Link to shop

### CTA

**Explore Hair Care Products**

---

## 9. FAQ

### Purpose

Answer common questions, remove objections and support long-tail SEO.

The FAQ should address the questions visitors normally have before contacting the clinic.

### Content

* SEO-focused section headline
* Supporting subheadline

### FAQ Categories

#### Hair Loss

Questions about causes, progression and diagnosis.

#### Hair Transplant

Questions about procedure, pain, grafts and suitability.

#### Results

Questions about natural results, growth timeline and expectations.

#### Recovery

Questions about downtime, washing, work and exercise.

#### Cost

Questions about treatment pricing and consultation.

#### Clinic & Consultation

Questions about appointments, doctors and the assessment process.

### Format

* Category navigation
* Short category description
* Accordion questions

---

## 10. Final Call to Action

### Purpose

Convert visitors who have reached the end of the page and now have enough information and trust to take the next step.

The final CTA should feel simple and low commitment.

### Content

* Strong conversion-focused headline
* Short supporting subheadline
* Primary CTA:
  **Start Free Hair Check**
* Secondary CTA:
  **Chat on WhatsApp**
* Trust reminders

  * Private
  * No obligation
  * Reviewed by RR
  * Quick response

### Suggested Message

**Not sure which treatment is right for you?**

Send us your photos and let RR help you understand your options.
